Open the tool

Will this housing scheme be allowed, and will a funder back it?

Funding-Fit Memo checks a site and a building option against Vancouver's zoning, the Province's rules, City policy, design standards and the funding programs, all at once, before the first drawing. It writes a short memo with three answers and shows every rule it used.

Open the tool with a walkthrough See two options compared

Three steps.

1 Pick a site

Choose one of the example sites or type in your own: the address, the zoning district, the lot size, the trees, who owns it. Each field has an (i) that explains what it is for.

2 Describe the option

Storeys, height, floor area, the unit mix, rents, who will own it, which funders you have in mind. "Not sure" is an honest answer: the memo marks it as uncertain instead of guessing.

3 Run the rules

Press Run the rules. The memo appears beside the form with its three answers, the massing drawn in plan, section and 3D, and a table of every clause that applied.

Change a number, run again, and compare the two runs side by side. Same inputs and the same rules always give the same memo; a stamp on each one says which version of each rule it read.

What the memo answers.

1 Is it allowed?

Outright, or by rezoning.

Density and height against the district schedule, the rezoning policy that applies, the provincial transit rules, the family-unit and tree by-laws.

2 Does it fit the funders?

Yes, no, or what is missing.

The Community Housing Fund, BC Builds and the others, tested against their published criteria: proponent, rent mix, accessible units, site control, lease.

3 What is still unsure?

Each open item, with a named person.

The architect, the development manager or the planner, and the document they need to check.

Under the answers: the drawings, the clauses applied with what the option proposes against what each clause permits, the full ledger with every number and its source, and the inputs as read. The memo downloads as a page you can print or send.

Try it on a real case.

998 E 19th Ave, Vancouver: six storeys of social housing on church land, approved by Council in July 2024. The inputs come from the City's public report; the placeholders say they are placeholders.
