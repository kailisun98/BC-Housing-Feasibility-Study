Label the uncertain parcels

40 parcels the classifier (ffm/parcel_shapes.py) is least sure of, from the City's parcel polygons (Open Government Licence – Vancouver). North is up in every thumbnail. Tick the shape you would call it; a note helps when none of the words fit, or when the parcel is not what the City draws (a consolidated site, a road closure). The draft stays in this browser until you download it. The classes: rectangle (four corners, square-ish), near rectangle (a bevelled corner, a slight skew, a short jog), trapezoid (four sides, not square-cornered), L shape (six corners, one inward), flag lot (a narrow strip leads to a wider body), irregular (anything else: triangles, curves, many sides), multi part (more than one piece).
