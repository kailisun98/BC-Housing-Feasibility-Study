RT-9 DISTRICT SCHEDULE (SYNTHETIC EVALUATION DOCUMENT)

This document is written by hand for evaluating First Read's rule reader. It is modelled on the layout of a
City of Vancouver district schedule but RT-9 is not a real district and no figure below is a real regulation.
Consolidated to 15 March 2026.

1 Intent
This schedule is intended to encourage the retention of character buildings and to permit two-family and
multiple dwellings compatible with the scale of the neighbourhood.

4 Regulations

4.3 Height
4.3.1 The maximum height of a building shall be 10.7 m, and a building shall not exceed 3 storeys.
4.3.2 The Director of Planning may permit a height of up to 12.2 m where a character house is retained and
the additional height does not cast shadow on a neighbouring yard.

4.4 Front Yard
4.4.1 A front yard with a minimum depth of 6.1 m shall be provided, except that where a lane does not abut
the rear of the site the minimum depth shall be 7.3 m.

4.5 Side Yards
4.5.1 A side yard with a minimum width of 1.5 m shall be provided on each side of the building.

4.6 Rear Yard
4.6.1 A rear yard with a minimum depth of 10.7 m shall be provided.

4.7 Floor Space Ratio
4.7.1 The floor space ratio shall not exceed 0.60.
4.7.2 The Director of Planning may permit an increase in the floor space ratio to a maximum of 0.75 where a
character house is retained and renovated, and to 0.85 where the character house is also designated as
protected heritage property.

4.8 Site Coverage
4.8.1 The maximum site coverage for buildings shall be 45 percent of the site area.
