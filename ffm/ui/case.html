The case

998 E 19th Ave.
Six storeys on church land.

Approved by Council on 25 July 2024. The inputs come from the City's public referral report and the Shape Your City page; the placeholders say they are placeholders. The tool reproduces the City's numbers: FSR 2.95 over 1.20, 36% family units over 35%, 20 trees required against 17.

1 · Is it allowed?

Not outright. By rezoning: yes, with conditions.

RM-1 outright FSR 1.20 and 10.7 m against the proposed 2.95 and 22.3 m. Eligible as 100% social housing under the Interim Rezoning Policy; the height is above the staff benchmark of 19.9 to 21.3 m and is negotiated.

2 · Does it fit the funders?

Not yet confirmed.

BC Builds needs the share of units at least 20% below market. The Community Housing Fund needs the rent-tier split, the accessible unit count and the land tenure. Its 2025 call was cancelled in February 2026.

3 · What is still unsure?

Four open items, each with a named person.

Two conditions attach to the rezoning and two figures are missing for the funders. Nothing fails; nothing is guessed.

03Revisions

Three memos on one site. Each numbered, each dated.
