Who it's for

Three readers.
One memo.

Housing societies, co-ops, churches and First Nations build homes that rent below market, with no development profit to absorb a wrong guess. The people around one of their sites read different documents at different times. The memo is the one thing they all read.

02What each reader does

Three flows through the same tool.

The tool is laid out around these, and its top bar has a switch, Manager · Architect · Reader, that picks the layout: the memo with the inputs beside it, the forms and the ledger open with Revit at hand, or the memo alone as on paper. The filled step is the one each reader came for.

The development manager

Site in, memo out. Before any money goes to design.

Flow 3

The board, the planner, the funder

PDF in, decision out. No briefing.

Open the memo as a reader
  1. 1
    Reads page one cold. Address, option, revision, rules date, then the three answers with the rules that decide them.PDF · page 1
  2. 2
    Reads page two. The open items, each with a person. A planner looks for the uncertain rules; a funder's officer for the missing figures.PDF · page 2
  3. 3
    Follows a reference. Every rule is A1 to E4; every source has a number and a URL.PDF · clauses, sources
  4. 4
    Decides. The pre-development money is voted, or not. The memo is dated, so a later reader can tell whether it is still true.the board gate

03What stays with people

The tool checks. It never decides.

A chat answer is not citable, repeatable or dated. The memo is the opposite of a guess, and the judgements are handed to the people whose judgements they are.

  1. 01Site facts

    Confirming what is true of the lot.

    The transit-oriented tier is read from the City's map by a person, not the tool.

  2. 02The option

    Choosing what to test.

    Storeys, mix, tenure and the program to chase are the provider's call.

  3. 03The massing

    Judging the picture.

    The massing is a picture of the rules, not a design. Whether it is a good building is the architect's.

  4. 04Uncertain rules

    Interpreting what the engine cannot.

    A CD-1 negotiation, a waived charge, a benchmark height: named and handed over.

  5. 05The decision

    Whether to proceed.

    The board votes. The memo is what it votes on, not who votes.

  6. 06The stamp

    Anything that gets stamped.

    A person checks the rule table before it is data, and the numbers before the revision goes on.
